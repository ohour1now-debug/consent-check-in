Consent Check-in

Please read

  • Consent can be withdrawn at any moment, by words or by actions, regardless of what this app shows. If someone says stop, hesitates, or goes quiet, stop.
  • This app is a communication tool only. It is not legal proof of consent and doesn't replace talking to each other.
  • A person who is intoxicated or impaired, asleep or unconscious, pressured or coerced, or under 18 cannot consent.
  • Consent to one thing isn't consent to everything, and consent now isn't consent later. Sessions expire and never carry over.
  • Privacy: use nicknames only. Names, choices and notes stay in memory and are wiped when the session ends. Only event timestamps are stored, and you can delete them.
